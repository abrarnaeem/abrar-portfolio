EducationBFA Film & Television — National College of Arts
BasedLahore, Pakistan

I'm Abrar Naeem, a filmmaker and post-production generalist with over 8 years of shaping images that resonate and endure. My work spans documentary, short film, commercial, and institutional production, grounded in a fine arts foundation from the National College of Arts in Lahore.

I currently serve as Lead VFX Artist at Narsun Studios and teach Advanced Post-Production to BFA students at NCA, roles that keep me engaged with both evolving tools and enduring craft. I move fluidly across the post-production pipeline, focusing on building cohesive, intentional visual experiences.

My approach is rooted in observation and precision. I focus on identifying what a piece needs, then refining it through a structured, iterative process until the image, rhythm, and sound align with clarity and purpose.
